# V9 Production Credit Risk and Decision Intelligence Report

V9 completes the functional portfolio by training independent application PD pipelines for Cash Loan, BNPL and Business Loan. Each pipeline has its own preprocessing, chronological development and out-of-time samples, validation metrics, artifact and governance record. Cash Loan and BNPL pass the synthetic validation thresholds. Business Loan remains under review because the available synthetic sample and out-of-time discriminatory power are insufficient for production approval.

The portfolio optimizer allocates a simulated VND 1 trillion capacity across products while enforcing concentration, weighted bad-rate, expected credit loss and profitability constraints. The recommended allocation is 50% Cash Loan, 10% BNPL and 40% Business Loan, subject to the Business Loan model review and live-data calibration.

The governance command center records model materiality, owner, independent validator, validation status, deployment stage, approval body, review date and findings. Customer-outcome monitoring covers affordability, high debt burden, repeat borrowing, complaint proxies, hardship proxies and payment-contact suppression. The Risk Committee pack converts these results into named decisions and accountable actions.

All data and results are synthetic. This is not an official TNEX system, is not affiliated with TNEX and must not be interpreted as production validation or an investment decision.
